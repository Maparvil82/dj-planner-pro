## Prompts finales

Cada imagen se generó por separado concatenando el prompt común y su escena. Herramienta integrada; no se utilizó la API ni el CLI.

### Prompt común

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: production mobile app background photograph, DJ Planner Pro authentication screen. Generate one standalone vertical portrait photo, aspect ratio 2:3, no phone frame or interface. Cohesive editorial music photography: authentic candid moment, tactile film grain, imperfect real textures, understated warm honey/amber highlights, charcoal and warm neutral surroundings, soft available light, restrained saturation. Feels like independent music magazine photography, never glossy AI advertising. Composition for a tall mobile screen: main focal subject centered horizontally within the middle 60% width and located in the upper quarter of the full image, face or focal detail around 20-25% from top; bottom half visually quiet, dark, softly out of focus because a form will overlay it. Keep uppermost 8% uncluttered for navigation. No writing, logos, watermark, interface, purple neon, glowing effects, collages, fake text, beauty retouching or unnaturally smooth skin.
